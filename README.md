**Influencer Engagement & Sponsorship Coordination Platform**
A full-stack web application that connects Sponsors and Influencers for managing advertising campaigns, sponsorship requests, and influencer collaborations. The platform enables sponsors to create campaigns, send ad requests, and track promotions, while influencers can discover campaigns, negotiate deals, and manage sponsorship opportunities.

**🚀 Features**
👨‍💼 Admin Dashboard


Monitor users, campaigns, and ad requests


View platform statistics


Flag inappropriate users or campaigns


Manage overall platform activities


🏢 Sponsor Functionalities


Create, update, and delete campaigns


Search influencers based on niche and reach


Send sponsorship/ad requests


Track campaign progress


Manage budgets and campaign visibility


🌟 Influencer Functionalities


View public campaigns


Accept or reject ad requests


Negotiate sponsorship payment amounts


Update public profile


Search campaigns based on category and budget


📢 Campaign & Ad Request Management


Public and private campaigns


Campaign goals and descriptions


Ad request tracking with status updates


Messaging and negotiation support
